# SIH26007 – Problem Statement

## Title

**Safe and Efficient Operation of Mine Vehicles in Fog and Low-Visibility Conditions in Open Cast Iron Ore Mines**

## Problem

Open-cast iron ore mines use large vehicles for transporting materials and carrying out different mining operations.

During fog and other low-visibility conditions, the driver's ability to visually identify nearby vehicles, obstacles and road conditions can be reduced.

This can make vehicle operation more difficult and increase the possibility of unsafe situations.

## Need

There is a need for a system that can provide the vehicle operator with additional information about the surroundings during low-visibility conditions.

The system should be able to use available sensing and vehicle information, understand the surrounding situation and provide useful warnings to the operator.

## Project Objective

The objective of our project is to develop a safety-oriented system that helps mine vehicle operators maintain better situational awareness during fog and low-visibility conditions.

The proposed system combines multiple sources of information, evaluates possible collision risks and provides appropriate warnings to the operator.

## Key Requirements

The proposed system focuses on:

* Detecting nearby vehicles and obstacles
* Monitoring vehicle movement
* Considering visibility conditions
* Estimating possible collision risk
* Providing timely warnings
* Recording important safety events
* Identifying repeated high-risk locations

## Proposed Outcome

The project aims to support safer and more efficient operation of mine vehicles by providing additional situational information to the operator when normal visibility is reduced.
